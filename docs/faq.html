Questions and answers

Short, honest answers about what Masla-e-Karachi is and is not.

About the project

It is an anonymous civic complaint and accountability platform for the people of Karachi. You report a problem with a photo and a location, the responsible department must answer, and departments are ranked on real data.

Privacy and safety

No. We never ask for your name, phone number or email, and you do not need to log in to report.

Reporting and results

Civic problems such as open gutters, broken roads, garbage, water and sewerage, electricity, gas and street lights. Sensitive problems like illegal liquor dens, drug spots and bhatta (extortion) are handled with extra care.

If nothing happens for 7 days, the problem is marked Overdue, a senior officer is emailed and the department’s ranking goes down.

The fix needs proof, an after-photo with a matching location. Then nearby residents confirm that it is really fixed.

No. Rankings use data only: resolution rate, average days, pending, overdue and reopened problems.
