Every day Karachi sees problems that nobody reports

Most people see them, but very few complain. They fear their name will be exposed, they do not know which department to go to, and they rarely get a reply.

  1. 01

    Open gutters and manholes

    A missing cover is a danger for every child, bike and car that passes by. Someone sees it, but no one reports it.

  2. 02

    Broken roads and potholes

    Damaged roads slow everyone down and cause accidents, yet there is no simple way to say where and how bad.

  3. 03

    Uncollected garbage

    Piles that stay for days. Which department should clear them? Many residents do not know.

  4. 04

    Water and sewerage

    Leaking pipes, overflowing sewers and water shortages.

  5. 05

    Electricity, gas and street lights

    Dark streets, faulty lines and gas problems that go unreported.

  6. 06

    Sensitive problems

    Illegal liquor dens, drug spots and bhatta (extortion). Handled with extra care: the exact location and photo are never made public.

One place to report, see and follow up

Masla-e-Karachi turns a quiet complaint into a public record. The responsible department is named, its answer is visible, and the result is checked by the community.

From a photo to a fixed problem in 4 steps

  1. Report

    Anyone reports a problem in four quick steps: category, location, photo or video, and details. A secret tracking code is given instead of an account.

  2. Moderation

    A moderator reviews every report before it goes public. Faces and number plates are blurred.

  3. Department replies

    The verified officer of the responsible department answers from an official dashboard. If nothing happens for 7 days, the problem is marked Overdue.

  4. Community confirms

    The fix needs proof, an after-photo, and nearby residents confirm that it is really fixed.

  • No account, only a code

    Instead of a login, you get a secret tracking code to follow your report.

  • Photo metadata is removed

    Hidden details such as location and device (EXIF) are designed to be stripped from photos before anything is shown.

    Your IP address is designed to be stored only as a scrambled hash, never as plain text.
