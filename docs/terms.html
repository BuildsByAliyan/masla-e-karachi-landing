About these terms

These terms explain how Masla-e-Karachi may be used and what may and may not be posted. By using the platform you agree to them.

Masla-e-Karachi is an independent, non-political platform. It is not a government service and it does not speak for any department.

Status of the platform

The real platform is under development. This landing website only introduces the project. Features described here are planned and may change before launch.

What you may and may not post

Please keep every report honest, respectful and about a civic problem.

You may post

  • A real problem you saw in Karachi
  • A clear photo or short video of the problem
  • The location and a few honest details
  • Facts: what you saw, where and when

You may not post

  • Names, phone numbers or addresses of people
  • Accusations against a person, or political attacks
  • Hate, threats, abuse or false reports
  • Private photos, or faces you cannot blur
  • Spam, adverts or anything unrelated

Moderation and removal

Every report is reviewed by a moderator before it becomes public. A moderator may edit, blur, hide or remove content that breaks these terms, is false or could put someone in danger.

If you believe something should be removed, contact us with the link and the reason. We will review it.

Anonymity and your responsibility

You can report without giving your name, phone number or email. You stay responsible for what you post. Do not include details that identify you or other people.

Your tracking code is the only way to follow your report. If you lose it, it cannot be recovered.

Department replies and accuracy

Reports are statements by members of the public and have not been proved by us. Replies come from the verified officers of departments. We show what is submitted and do not guarantee that it is complete or correct.

Rankings show public data about reports. They are not a verdict on any department or person.

This is not an emergency service

If life is in danger, call 15 first. Do not use this website to ask for urgent help.

Your content

You keep the rights to the photos, videos and text you post. By posting, you allow Masla-e-Karachi to show them on the platform and to share them with the relevant department or authority so that the problem can be handled.

Limits of responsibility

We work to keep the platform safe and useful, but we cannot force any department to act and we do not promise that a problem will be fixed. The platform is provided as it is, to the extent allowed by law.

Law

All content must follow the laws of Pakistan. These terms will be reviewed by a qualified lawyer before the real platform launches.

Changes to these terms

We may update these terms as the platform grows. The date at the top of this page shows the latest version.
