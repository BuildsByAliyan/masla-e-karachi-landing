Anonymous by design

You can speak up without being known

Anonymity is the core promise of Masla-e-Karachi. Here is what we do to protect you, in simple words.

The short version

No name, no phone, no email

You report without an account. We never ask who you are.

A secret tracking code

You follow your report with a code, not a login.

Checked before it goes public

A moderator reviews every report. Faces and number plates are blurred.

We never ask for
  • Your name
  • Your phone number
  • Your email address
  • A login or password to report

How we protect you

No technical words needed.

  1. Photo details are removed

    Photos hide small details inside the file, such as the phone model, GPS and the time. Our server strips all of it before the photo is saved.

    Phone model GPS in the file Time taken Clean photo

    Removed by our server before anything is saved

  2. A secret code, not an account

    After reporting you get a tracking code like the one shown. Use it to follow your report without ever creating an account.

    We keep only a scrambled version of the code. The trade-off: if you lose it, nobody can recover it for you.

    MEK-8F3K-92

    Example code. Only its hash is stored.

  3. IP addresses are hashed

    To stop spam we need to notice when one connection sends many reports. We store only a scrambled hash of the IP address, never the address itself.

    203.0.113.25 → 9f2c…a41e

    Turned into a scrambled hash (example address)

  4. Every report is moderated

    A moderator checks each report before it goes public. Faces and number plates are blurred so that nobody is identified by accident.

    Under review → Public

    Moderator checks and blurs

  5. Sensitive reports stay private

    For problems like illegal liquor dens, drug spots and bhatta (extortion), the exact location and photo are not shown to the public. Only moderators and the relevant authority, such as the police or anti-narcotics, can see them.

    Hidden from the public

    Seen only by moderators and the relevant authority

About this website and our limits

A lost code cannot be recovered

We keep only a scrambled version of the code. The trade-off: if you lose it, nobody can recover it for you.

  • This landing website does not ask for or store any personal information, and it has no accounts. It does not use cookies or trackers.
  • Fonts are served from our own server, so your browser does not contact Google or any other outside service to show them when a page opens.
  • Anonymity also depends on what you share. Please avoid showing your own face, your house number or other details that could identify you in a photo or in the description.
  • The real platform is still under development. Its full privacy policy will be finalised, with legal advice, before launch.
