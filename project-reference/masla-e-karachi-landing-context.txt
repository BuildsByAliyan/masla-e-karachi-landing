# PART A - THE REAL PROJECT: MASLA-E-KARACHI
####################################################################################################


## NAME
  Masla-e-Karachi

## MEANING
  'Karachi's problem/issue' in Urdu. Instantly understood by any Karachi resident as being about
  civic issues.

## TAGLINE
  Masla batao, hisaab lo (Report the problem, hold them accountable)

## REAL WEBSITE DOMAIN
  maslaekarachi.pk (owner's final decision; earlier notes said .com or .pk). The real site will live
  here when launched.

## TARGET AUDIENCE
  Residents of Karachi, Pakistan.

## PREVIOUS NAME NOTE
  The name 'Basilaay' was considered earlier but dropped because it was hard to say and write.

## WHY IT IS BEING BUILT
  - Ordinary people in Karachi see civic problems every day but do not complain.
  - Reasons they do not complain: fear their name will be exposed; they do not know which department
    to complain to; no reply after complaining; no way to see which departments actually work.
  - Examples of problems: open/broken gutter covers (manholes), broken roads and potholes,
    uncollected garbage, water and sewerage problems, electricity/gas/street-light issues, and
    sensitive categories like illegal liquor dens, drug spots and 'bhatta' (extortion) spots.

## WHAT IT DOES
  - Any citizen anonymously uploads a photo/short video (max 30 sec) with location and details. No
    name, phone, email or login needed.
  - The problem becomes public after moderator review (map + list).
  - Each problem shows the government department responsible (KMC, KWSC, K-Electric, SSGC, Sindh
    Solid Waste Management Board, Traffic Police, etc.).
  - The department's verified officer must respond from an Official Dashboard; everyone can see
    whether they responded.
  - Resolution needs proof (after-photo) and community confirmation: nearby residents vote whether
    it is really fixed.
  - If a department does nothing for 7 days, the problem gets an 'Overdue' flag, a senior officer is
    emailed, and the department's ranking drops.
  - Departments are ranked publicly on data (resolution %, average days, pending/overdue, reopened).
    Likes are NOT used in ranking.

## CORE GOALS
  - Give citizens a voice without fear.
  - Make government departments accountable.
  - Create public pressure so public money is used properly.
  - Give public recognition to departments and people who actually do the work.

## REALISTIC LIMITS
  - The website cannot force any department to act; its power is public transparency.
  - It is NOT anti-government and NOT political; it is a non-political platform for everyone.
  - Pakistan Citizen Portal (government app) already exists. Difference here: anonymous complaints,
    public map, public ranking.
  - It is NOT an emergency service. Every page must say: 'If life is in danger, call 15 first.'

## STORY EXAMPLE
  Ahmed (32, Gulshan-e-Iqbal Block 13-D) sees an open gutter cover. He opens the site via a WhatsApp
  link, reports in a 4-step form (Category, Location pin, Photo/Video, Details) with no name, gets a
  secret tracking code like MEK-8F3K-92, moderators approve, the problem appears on the map as a red
  pin, neighbours click 'I am affected too', KWSC officer replies, fixes it, uploads an after-photo,
  residents confirm, pin turns green, it appears in Success Stories and KWSC's ranking improves.

## REPORT FORM 4 STEPS
  - Category
  - Location (GPS or manual map pin)
  - Photo or up to 30-second video
  - Details in Urdu or English

## PRIVACY AND SAFETY DESIGN
  - No name/phone/email requested; only a secret tracking code (only its hash is stored).
  - Photo EXIF metadata (phone model, GPS, time) is stripped by the server.
  - IP address is stored only as a hash (for spam control).
  - Every complaint is moderated before going public; faces and number plates are blurred.
  - Sensitive complaints (liquor/drugs/bhatta): exact location and photo are NOT public; only
    moderators and the relevant department (police / anti-narcotics) see them.
  - Cloudflare Turnstile (anti-bot) and rate limiting against spam.
  - Legal risks (defamation, PECA cyber-crime law in Pakistan) require a lawyer before launch.
    Political-party ranking is NOT in phase 1.
  - Tracking-code tradeoff: if a user loses the code it cannot be recovered (optional login could
    fix this later).

## CITIZEN FIX FLOW
  Not only departments fix problems. Any user can press 'This Problem Is Fixed' on a complaint page
  and upload an after-photo. Status becomes 'Citizen claims it's fixed - verification pending'. GPS
  of after-photo must match the original; a moderator checks; nearby/affected residents vote. If
  confirmed, status is 'Confirmed Resolved (Community)' and pin turns green. A 'resolved_by' field
  (department / community / unknown) ensures department rankings count ONLY work done by the
  department, but the department's Overdue flag stays if it failed to act in 7 days.

## RANKING PHASES
  phase_1: Departments only
  phase_2: Areas (towns/union councils)
  phase_3: Political parties, only with enough data and legal advice, labeled 'Public data, not a
  verdict'

# QUICK SUMMARY
####################################################################################################

Masla-e-Karachi is an anonymous civic-complaint and accountability platform for Karachi residents:
report a problem with a photo and location without giving your name, the responsible government
department must respond, the community confirms the fix, and departments are publicly ranked on real
data. The landing website (this repo, plain HTML/CSS/JS on GitHub Pages from /docs) introduces the
project in English and sends visitors to the real site at maslaekarachi.pk.
